01 / CURRENT PROJECT

RoleBridge AI
The Path Changer.

A career-mobility product concept for mid-career technology professionals who need a realistic route from current capabilities to an aspiring role.

ROLEBRIDGE AI · 001In development

A clearer path
from here to there.

RoleBridge goes beyond recommending content. The product thesis is to help learners understand their starting point, map a path to a realistic target role, and get timely support when progress stalls.

Product discoveryPersonalized pathwaysGenAI supportHuman coaching
Explore the product thinking
Problem

Discovery suggested the challenge is not simply finding content; it is connecting learning to a realistic target role and getting useful help at the moment of friction.

Core experience

A learner-verified five-vector skills profile, personalized learning path, scoped AI help, and contextual human coaching when judgment or reassurance matters.

Product ownership

My focus is product strategy and validation: problem framing, profile concept, experience requirements, prioritization, and decisions behind two product pivots.

Next validation

Test path relevance, completion, skill progression, profile corrections, response quality, privacy controls, and coaching economics with a broader pilot.
